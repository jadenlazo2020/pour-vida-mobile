Questions, Answered

What does "dry hire" mean?

Dry hire means you purchase and provide the alcohol, and we provide everything else: professional bartending, tools, mixers and garnishes (depending on your package), setup, and breakdown. It keeps your costs down and lets you choose exactly what's poured.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Central Valley, including Fresno, Clovis, Visalia, Madera, Merced, Hanford, and surrounding cities. Traveling a little farther? Reach out and we'll let you know if a travel fee applies.

How much alcohol should I buy?

Don't stress about it. Once you book, we'll send a personalized shopping guide based on your guest count, event length, and menu.

Do you offer mocktails?

Yes! We can make zero-proof versions of any signature drink, or build a fully alcohol-free menu for your event.

How far in advance should I book?

Weekends during wedding and quince season fill up quickly, so we recommend inquiring 2–6 months ahead. For smaller events, reach out anytime and we'll check availability.

How do I reserve my date?

After we send your custom proposal, a signed agreement and deposit officially hold your date.
