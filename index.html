Questions

Don't see yours? Send us a message on Instagram at @pourvida_mobilebar.

What does "dry hire" mean?

You buy and provide the alcohol. We bring the bartending, tools, mixers and garnishes (depending on your package), and handle setup and breakdown. You pay store prices for alcohol and choose exactly what gets poured.

What areas do you serve?

The Central Valley, including Fresno, Clovis, Visalia, Madera, Merced, Hanford and nearby cities. Farther out? Ask us. A travel fee may apply.

How much alcohol should I buy?

After you book, we send a shopping guide based on your guest count, how long the event runs and your menu.

Do you offer mocktails?

Yes. Any signature drink can be made zero-proof, or we can build a menu that's entirely alcohol-free.

How far in advance should I book?

Weekend weddings and quinceañeras book up first, so reach out 2 to 6 months ahead. For smaller events, ask anytime and we'll check the calendar.

How do I reserve my date?

Once you approve your proposal, a signed agreement and deposit hold the date.
